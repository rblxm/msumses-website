Academic & Career Support

We host a weekly Help Room in STEM 2004 at 5:30 PM. Our Education Chair and other board members are available to assist with coursework, lab reports, and conceptual questions. Additionally, we host resume workshops and guest speakers from both academia and industry.

Research & Materials Databases

Check out some of our most frequently visited tools and databases.

  • PTable: An interactive tool featuring comprehensive physical, atomic, and chemical property data for all elements.
  • Materials Project: An open-access database providing calculated properties and apps for thousands of known and predicted materials.
  • ICSD: The Inorganic Crystal Structure Database—the world's largest database for fully identified inorganic crystal structures.
  • Phonon Website: A visual and interactive tool for exploring lattice vibrations and phonon dispersion relations.
  • National Nuclear Data Center: A database for nuclear structure, reaction, and decay data hosted by Brookhaven National Laboratory.

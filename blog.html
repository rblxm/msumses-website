Welcome Spartans!

If you're new on campus or back for another round, MSES extends a warm welcome. I hope you are as excited for this upcoming semester as we are!

I remember one of first few days into school- being so excited when I finnaly bumped into another MSE major. As a freshman in materials science, it can be difficult to feel like part of a group when you don't have many MSE classes. So, one of our biggest goals is to foster that community. The friends I've made through this major have been life-changing. I hope you'll come join us!

We will be kicking off our weekly meetings on September 7th with an introduction to the major and the club. The helproom will be open as well.

Catch'ya there,

What is MSES?

The Materials Science and Engineering Society (MSES) at Michigan State University is dedicated to fostering a community of students passionate about the materials field. We provide professional development opportunities, academic support, and a social network for future engineers and scientists. We also do lots of fun MSE crafts and social events!

Whether you are interested in metals, polymers, ceramics, or more, we would love to have ya!
